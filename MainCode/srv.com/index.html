SRV Kinetic
Screen Protector Cutting Machine

As a new model of screen protector cutting machine, the SRV Kinetic is designed to meet the needs of devices across all screen sizes — from small smartwatches to large PC monitors. It offers precise and reliable cutting performance for a wide range of devices. It also comes with enhanced convenience features, including mobile app connectivity and optional automatic parameter adjustment, making operation easier, faster, and more efficient.

Screen Protector Cutting Machine

As SRV’s latest device, the SRV Kinetic Screen Protector Machine not only supports a wider cutting range but also introduces more innovative features. It offers optional automatic adjustment of cutting parameters and is capable of cutting 7H and 9H super-hard materials with precision and efficiency.
